## Premium Standard

## Anti-Generic Standard

- 3D must enhance meaning, not distract
- decorative 3D must still support identity
- provide fallback behavior

## Copy Standard
- avoid cliché startup phrases
- avoid fake visionary language
- keep copy premium and specific
- keep the hero sharp and memorable
- feature/value language should sound credible

## Frontend Standard
- think in systems, not screenshots
- think in components, states, and responsiveness
- think in motion timing and performance costs
- propose realistic implementation approaches
- balance spectacle with usability
